```markdown
You are extracting NFL betting picks from X/Twitter for a betting-research database. Today is Saturday 2026-11-21; the slate is NFL 2026 Week 11.

Search the timelines of these four accounts for posts from 2026-11-16 through now:
- @thepowerrank (Ed Feng)
- @thejoeholkashow (Joe Holka)
- @thepropdealer (The Prop Dealer)
- Marc Gartenberg (find his handle; he posts NFL picks)

Only these 12 games count (IND@HOU on Thursday is over; ignore it):
MIA@BUF, NO@CHI, TEN@DAL, TB@DET, ARI@KC, PIT@PHI, BAL@CAR, NYJ@LAC, LV@DEN, JAX@NYG, MIN@SF (Mexico City, Sunday night), CIN@WAS (Monday night).

For each post, read the author's full self-reply thread, every attached image/graphic, and any linked video or article. Do not rely on the first tweet alone.

Return exactly TWO things, in this order, both in the chat:

### 1) One ```csv code block
Header row exactly:
`author,tweet_url,bet_type,team_or_market,market,selection,line,odds,rationale`

One row per individual bet.
- `author`: the X handle without @.
- `tweet_url`: the URL of the post the pick came from, format `https://x.com/<handle>/status/<id>`. Never truncate it.
- `bet_type`: one of `spread`, `total`, `moneyline`, `team_total`, `player_prop`, `anytime_td`, `first_td`, `parlay_leg`, `trend`.
- `team_or_market`: full team name for sides/moneylines; `AWAY@HOME` for totals; the player's full name for props.
- `market`: e.g. `spread`, `total`, `passing yards`, `receptions`, `anytime td`.
- `selection`: the side exactly as posted (`OVER`, `UNDER`, team name, `YES`).
- `line`: the number exactly as posted; blank if none.
- `odds`: American odds exactly as posted; blank if none. Never invent a price.
- `rationale`: start with `AWAY@HOME | <post time in ISO UTC> |`, then a short paraphrase of the stated reason. If the pick is part of a parlay, say "parlay leg" here and use `bet_type` `parlay_leg`.

Do not include picks for any other week, college games, or futures. Do not guess a line or price that the post does not show.

### 2) A short plain-text summary
For each of the four accounts: the number of Week 11 picks found, or "no Week 11 picks found" plus what you checked (the date range and the kinds of posts).
```
