# Claude Recommendation Ledger — 2026 Season

Standing record of what Claude proposed vs. what Andy actually placed, so divergences can be graded
after the fact. Started 2026-09-20 at Andy's request.

**How to grade a divergence.** A parlay dies on its weakest leg, so "did diverging help?" is only
answerable by checking which legs hit:

- **Divergence HURT** — every leg Claude proposed hit, but an added/kept/swapped-in leg missed. The
  Claude ticket would have paid; the placed one didn't.
- **Divergence IRRELEVANT** — a leg common to both tickets missed. Both lose regardless.
- **Divergence HELPED** — every leg hit. The placed ticket paid more than Claude's would have.

Record the verdict *and* the counterfactual dollar figures. One week proves nothing; the point is the
running tally.

## Method notes

- Prices are BEO unless stated. Proposals came off `docs/Player_Prop_Odds_Weekly/Week2/` boards
  parsed with `~/pp/beo.py`; Week 1 support stats from `~/pp/w1.json`, built from the repo's 16 ESPN
  Week 1 boxscores.
- Where a booked price differs from the board price at proposal time, the booked price governs the
  counterfactual.
- **BEO pricing behaviour, Week 2:** rounded *up* vs. the raw leg multiply on tickets with up to
  three doubled games (+10682→+11100, +4234→+4300, +2934→+3000, +12158→+12300), then **trimmed 15.4%**
  on the ticket carrying five legs in one game (+9942→+8400). Working hypothesis: the correlation cut
  starts somewhere above two legs per game. Worth testing again before assuming it generalises.
- **Pricing behaviour, Week 3 TNF:** BEO Prop Shop cut four same-script legs ~30% (naive +1418 → +1000)
  and priced an anti-correlated swap *above* naive (Bijan o80.5: +1454 → +1600). BEO Props Builder
  trimmed a 6-leg ~14% (+12,800 → +11000). The same 5 Tier 2 legs priced +1800 at BEO vs **+2584 at
  BKR** — BKR's middle ladder rungs run ~9 cents cheaper than BEO's standard lines on identical numbers.
