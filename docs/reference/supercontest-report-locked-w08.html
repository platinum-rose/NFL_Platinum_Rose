📋 How the SuperContest works
  • Five picks a week, against the contest's own point spreads (locked for the whole week). This report covers point-spread sides only.
  • Scoring: a win is 1 point, a push (lands exactly on the number) is ½, a loss is 0.
  • Why the contest line matters: the contest locks its lines early in the week, while sportsbook lines keep moving. When the contest gives our side a better number than the market does now, that is free value. When it gives a worse number, we are paying for it, and it matters most around 3 and 7, the most common winning margins.
