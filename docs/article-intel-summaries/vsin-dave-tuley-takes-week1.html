1. Executive Summary & Core Strategic Thesis

Veteran handicapper Dave Tuley executes his signature 'dogs-or-pass' quantitative philosophy across Week 1. Tuley prioritizes capturing points across key numbers 3, 4, and 7, highlighting live underdogs with strong trench continuity (Patriots +3.5, Browns +8, Buccaneers +3.5, Colts +3.5, Falcons +3.5). For the Melbourne international game, Tuley breaks from sides to target the Under 48.5.

“We don't need our dogs to win outright—we just need them to stay within one score. Grabbing the hook at +3.5 across Tampa Bay, New England, and Indy gives us tremendous mathematical protection.”
