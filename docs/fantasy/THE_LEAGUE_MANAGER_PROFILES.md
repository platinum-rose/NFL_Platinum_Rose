# The League Manager Profiles

Last updated: 2026-09-21

Use this as local context when writing weekly fantasy recaps for `2026 - The League`.

## Recap Voice

- Deadpan surreal league recap.
- Keep jokes original and fantasy-literate.
- Do not imitate or quote any specific comedian.
- Prefer short joke drops, literal fantasy logic, and punctuation-driven timing.
- Separate starter points from bench points.
- Do not roast scoreless starters who have not played yet; call them pending.
- Bench points can be recurring comedy, but vary the phrasing unless repetition is the joke.
