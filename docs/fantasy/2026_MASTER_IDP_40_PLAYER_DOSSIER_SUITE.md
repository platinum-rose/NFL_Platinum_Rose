## The IDP Evaluation Framework

These three signals underlie the expert consensus that produced the DraftSharks rankings powering this list:

**1. The Green Dot Signal:** The linebacker wearing the defensive helmet communicator is the one calling plays. He never leaves the field in sub-packages. This is the strongest single predictor of a 3-down, high-volume tackle floor.

**2. The Nickel Trap:** Modern NFL defenses run base personnel on fewer than 35% of snaps. A linebacker who comes off the field for Nickel/Dime packages logs 45–65% snap counts — not an IDP starter regardless of depth-chart listing.

**3. The Snap-Share Floor:** Target linebackers at or near 90%+ snap volume. Below 80%, weekly floor is unreliable. This applies even to players with strong tackle-per-snap efficiency if they aren't on the field enough.
