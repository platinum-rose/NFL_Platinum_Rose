I. Applicable Constitutional Provisions (The Pinned Rules)

The following provisions from the official pinned league constitution govern keeper eligibility and round placement:

"2. To keep, surrender a draft pick 2 rounds higher than keeper was drafted (e.g. keeping a player drafted in 2025, 3rd round will forfeit 1st round pick)."

"6. Late round (14+ round) draft picks kept the following year count as 14th round picks."

"9. If two players have the same value (both 7th round), owner must select one of the two to have a higher value (one 6th and one 7th)."
— Official Pinned League Constitution (Re-affirmed Aug 21, 2026)

In the 2025 pre-draft cycle, Manager Andy Rose initially submitted a proposed 12th-round keeper cost for Brock Bowers. Commissioner Amanda issued an official written ruling on the mandatory application of Rule 6:

Under this established precedent, any player drafted in Round 14 or later enters Year 1 of keeper tenure at a fixed baseline cost of Round 14.
