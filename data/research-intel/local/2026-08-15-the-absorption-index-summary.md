# 📊 The Absorption Index — Summary & Layman's Guide
**Author**: Karl Herbst (*Wireframe News*, August 12, 2026)  
**URL**: https://read.wireframenews.com/p/the-absorption-index  
**Topic**: AI Progress Velocity vs. Human Society Adaptation Capacity

---

## 💡 Core Premise
Instead of arguing about how "smart" or "scary" AI is getting, the author created a quantitative speedometer called **The Absorption Index**. 

It measures a simple race: **The Speed of AI Progress (Accelerants)** vs. **The Speed of Human Adaptation & Regulation (Brakes)**.

$$ \text{Absorption Index} = \frac{\text{Speed of AI Advance}}{\text{Human Capacity to Adapt \& Regulate}} $$

---

## 🔑 Key Concepts & Current Gauge Reading

### 1. The Benchmark Line (1.0)
* **Below 1.0**: Humans are barely keeping up with AI changes.
* **At 1.0**: Society and laws absorb AI shocks at the exact speed they arrive.
* **Above 1.0**: Humans fall behind, creating an unmanageable backlog of social, economic, and political disruptions.
* 📈 **Current Index Reading**: **0.7** *(and rising rapidly)*.

---

## 🏎️ The Gas Pedal (7 Accelerants Driving AI Forward)
1. **Accelerating Intelligence**: The time required for AI reasoning horizon to double dropped from 7 months to **3 months**.
2. **Infrastructure Capital**: Tech giants are spending over **$700 Billion** this year building AI data centers (Nvidia data-center revenue +68% to $193.7B).
3. **Venture Capital Dominance**: **86 cents out of every $1.00** of US venture capital in early 2026 went directly into AI startups (up from $0.65 in 2025).
4. **Recursive Code Generation**: Over **80% of the codebase** at leading AI labs is now written by AI itself.

---

## 🛑 The Brakes (4 Safety Mechanisms Slipping)
1. **Ineffective Legislation**: Out of ~1,200 US state AI bills introduced in 2025, only **12%** became law.
2. **Failing Safety Audits**: Independent panels gave top AI companies an overall existential safety grade of **D+** (highest score was C-).
3. **Delayed EU Rules**: Key EU AI Act rules governing AI in hiring, medicine, and policing were officially postponed until **2027–2028**.
4. **Optional Self-Regulation**: When OpenAI announced it was slowing research for safety, it immediately launched training on its next major model ("DOUG").

---

## 🎯 Bottom Line
The Absorption Index sits at **0.7** because technological capabilities are compounding exponentially while legal, institutional, and human absorption capacity is moving linearly.
