## LAC@BUF
projection: BUF 28, LAC 23

Splits are public on Buffalo (75% of tickets, 73% of money), with no sharp gap. The one exposure is on the still-live ticket 739211245 (BUF ML). LAC +7 is only Andy's small hedge against the Buffalo futures. The projected 51 points sit on the 50.5 total, which is why the two Over picks didn't make the card.

### What breaks it
Buffalo's two questionable receivers (DJ Moore, Keon Coleman) both sit and the offense narrows to Kincaid and Shakir. Or Herbert turns it over twice early and the game gets away from LA, turning a five-point Bills win into a 14-point one.

## CAR@CLE
projection: CLE 20, CAR 17

### Game script
This is a field-position game in Cleveland. The Browns' defense controls early downs, and Bryce Young, who has been sharper under the new play-calling, has to string together long drives with Tetairoa McMillan and a banged-up WR room (Coker and Legette both questionable). Deshaun Watson's offense leans on Quinshon Judkins and short throws. Watson's own legs near the goal line are the reason Chad Millman took his anytime TD at +330. Two field-goal trades and a single short-field touchdown decide it. Cleveland wins a low game at home, and the Schwesinger tackle leg (10 and 10 in weeks 1–2) should clear, because Carolina runs Chuba Hubbard 20+ times.

### Why the card leans this way
The money is on Cleveland. Only 22% of spread tickets back CLE but 48% of the money does, the biggest money-versus-tickets gap on the Sunday slate. The CLE moneyline shortened from +126 (Tue) to +120 even though 88% of moneyline tickets are on Carolina. Sharp or Square, Millman, VSiN and Jared Smith are on CLE, and Even Money has it as a Wong teaser leg (+2.5 → +8.5). The Under (tier 2) has Even Money and BettingPros behind it, and BettingPros' Erickson flags possible weather. The DK Predictions save is the one dissent: CAR moneyline at 57%.

### What breaks it
Young protects the ball and Carolina's back-to-back road trip doesn't show up. A Carolina lead forces Watson to throw 35+ times, which hurts both the CLE moneyline and the Under.

## NYJ@DET
projection: DET 26, NYJ 23

### Game script
Detroit comes in off extended rest after Thursday and scores through Jahmyr Gibbs (−531 anytime TD) and Amon-Ra St. Brown. The Jets have the matchup of the week, though: NYJ passing offense against the Detroit secondary grades HIGH at a 9.26 severity, the most lopsided number on the slate. The card's NYJ moneyline (+234, tier 1+2) is a price bet. The projection has Detroit winning. At +234 the Jets need to win about 30% of the time, and a HIGH secondary grade plus Action Network and Sharp or Square on the Jets makes a 30%+ upset chance reasonable. The market drifted toward New York all week: the Detroit moneyline went from −301 (Tue) to −274 with the spread flat. Over money is 51% against 25% of tickets, a 26-point sharp gap, which fits the high-scoring script here. Note that the live ticket 739211245 still has DET ML, so the card is on both sides of this game.

### What breaks it
Detroit's pass rush takes away the vertical shots and Gibbs runs out the clock. In that script both the Jets moneyline and the Wilson over lose together; they are correlated, not two separate edges.
