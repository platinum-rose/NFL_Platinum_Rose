## Podcast and YouTube hosts

### Steve Fezzik (Even Money; 5 ATS-contest picks + 2-unit plays)
Fezzik's week is a contest player's card: he takes points almost everywhere and fades teams he thinks the market overrates. His top-5 contest picks are **TB +3.5, SEA -2.5, LV +4.5, NYG +10.5**, plus **IND +6.5** (2u), on the view that Minnesota is "barely an above-average team" with poor yards per play on offense. On the Giants he argues 10 points is too many for a Houston team coming off back-to-back emotional games and international travel. He's on **ARI +4.5** (2u) because Denver's offense "stinks on the road", and pairs with Ross Tucker on **CIN +3** (2u, best bet). Totals: **Over 44.5 TB@CAR** (2u: Carolina's defense is weak but its offense is improving) and **Under 38.5 CLE@TEN** (2u: the Titans have "virtually no offense, especially outdoors").
- Moved (Sat BKR; Andy-supplied board): TB +3.5 → +3 (-119); LV +4.5 → +3 (+102); CIN +3 → CIN -2.5 (-118; now the other side); NYG +10.5 → +10 (-103); Under 38.5 → 37 (-113). ARI is +4.5 (-108); IND +6.5 (-108) is unchanged. TB/CAR total is 44.5, unchanged from his Over number; SEA is now -3 (-101). His old CIN +3 and LV +4.5 prices are gone.
- Also said (voice-verified transcripts): a **strong lean to Atlanta** on Tuesday, then on Thursday he passed on ATL at +2.5 ("I can't take plus two and a half"); a "strong lean" to **Seattle** on Tuesday (not a bet); his weekly-contest double-weight lean was CHI -2.5 (TNF, final). Thursday prop: a fourth-quarter parlay of IND +2.5, ARI +1.5 and LV +0.5 (fourth-quarter lines, not comparable to full-game numbers).

### Simon Hunter (Sharp or Square; "Simon Says" and the Sharp pick)
Hunter reads sharp money and situational spots, and he's on more underdogs than anyone this week: **TB +3.5** (bounce-back spot), **NO +3.5** (international favorites regress), **TEN +1.5** (sharp groups back Tennessee home dogs against a public Browns team), **CIN +3**, **MIA +4.5** (off a bye against a Jets team coming off a tough road game), **GB +3.5** (Jordan Love "playing at MVP level"), **LV +4.5**, **KC +2.5** (Mahomes as a dog, or tease to +8.5), **PHI +4.5** ("playing the number"). His one favorite is **HOU -9.5**, following sharp money against the Giants.
- Flags: he has **both DEN -4 and ARI +4.5** on different episodes, so he's neutral on that game. The HOU case was made against "Jameis Winston", but J.J. McCarthy starts. The LV case cites "Aaron Donald declining", and Donald isn't on the Rams' roster.

### Brandon Anderson (Action Network, "Brandon's Playground"; articles and YouTube = one opinion)
Anderson's favorite side is **DET -3**: his power ratings make Detroit a 5-point favorite. He takes **PIT -3** because Pittsburgh's rushing (3rd in DVOA) meets the league's worst run defense, and likes **MIN -2.5 in the second half** because the Vikings are 4-1 against the spread after halftime. **Under 50 SF@ATL** rests on San Francisco's short week before a bye, a depleted receiver room and an Atlanta offense that runs. Most of his card is Tight Ends Day props: **Kittle anytime TD** (8 TDs in 13 Purdy starts), **Parkinson anytime TD** (the Rams' red-zone TE, 9 TDs in 14 games), **Higbee 5+ catches**, **Hockenson 7+ catches** (Colts weak over the middle), and longshots **Cuevas, Redman, Barner and Spann-Ford** to score. He also has **McCaffrey 6+ / 7+ catches** (SF out of receivers) and **Watson and Golden 80+ receiving yards** against Detroit's secondary.
- Note: the MIN -2.5 is a second-half line, not comparable to the full game, and its "post-London fatigue" premise doesn't hold: the Colts were home in Week 6.
