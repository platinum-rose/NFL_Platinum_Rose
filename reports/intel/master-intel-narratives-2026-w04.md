## IND@WAS
projection: IND 26, WAS 20

### Game script
In London at 06:30 PT, Washington starts Marcus Mariota with Jayden Daniels out, Terry McLaurin doubtful and Rachaad White out. Bookmaker had pulled the Mariota, McLaurin and Jacory Croskey-Merritt prop menus at its 10/03 capture, which says the book itself isn't sure what this offense looks like. Indianapolis loses Keenan Allen (out on the 10/03 ESPN feed), so the targets concentrate on Josh Downs (72+ receiving yards at −106) and Tyler Warren (54+ at −105) on a MEDIUM secondary grade against a defense allowing 6.4 yards per play. Jonathan Taylor (91+ rushing at −102) runs into the Washington run defense the WAS backers lean on, so the Colts win through the air. Indianapolis by six: a cover of −4.5 by a point and a half, 46 total points.

### Why the card leans this way
Experts split 7–4 toward Washington: Action Network ("Mariota is a top-tier backup"), BettingPros, Ross Tucker and Doug Kezirian at +3.5, PFF and Travis Pulver of BettingPros on the moneyline, and Mike Florio (Commanders 24–20) on WAS; Brandon Anderson, Andrew Erickson and Jason Logan at −3.5 and Ian Undery of Action Network on the moneyline for IND. Splits: WAS has 68% of spread tickets but only 58% of the money; the IND moneyline has 70% of tickets and 83% of the money, short of a 15-point sharp gap. The projection (IND by 6) disagrees with the expert majority, but only by a point and a half against the number, so the lean is to pass the side and play the receiver: Brandon Anderson and Andrew Erickson both have Downs over 62.5 receiving, noting Washington has allowed the most wide-receiver fantasy points. The projected 46 sits a point under the 47 total; The over has four backers (BettingPros and Zachary Cohen of VSiN at 46.5, Ian Undery at 49.5, and a betting-trends account on X), mostly on the same 15–1 trend when Washington is a 3.5- to 9.5-point underdog.
