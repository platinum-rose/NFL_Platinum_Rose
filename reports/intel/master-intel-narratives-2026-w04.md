## IND@WAS
projection: IND 26, WAS 20

### Game script

Indianapolis by six: a cover of −4.5 by a point and a half, 46 total points.

Experts split 5–3 toward Washington, and that side was bet at +3.5: Action Network ("Mariota is a top-tier backup"), BettingPros, Ross Tucker, Doug Kezirian and Mike Florio (Commanders 24–20) on WAS; Brandon Anderson, Andrew Erickson and Jason Logan on IND −3.5. The projection (IND by 6) disagrees with the expert majority, but only by a point and a half against the number, so the lean is to pass the side and play the receiver: Brandon Anderson and Andrew Erickson both have Downs over 62.5 receiving, noting Washington has allowed the most wide-receiver fantasy points.
