## PIT@NO
projection: PIT 23, NO 20

### Game script
Paris, open-air Stade de France, with a forecast of 26 km/h wind gusting to 54 km/h. Aaron Rodgers runs a pass-heavy offense through a thin receiver room (Ben Skowronek out) and Jaylen Warren, with Rico Dowdle questionable. The wind trims deep shots and kicks more than Rodgers' short game. Pittsburgh's defense (#6 by EPA) should control a Saints offense whose 25.3 points a game lean on an 84% red-zone touchdown rate and turnover-fed short fields. Tyler Shough throws into a Steelers secondary missing Brandin Echols, with DeShon Elliott questionable, which is the Saints' best lever. A 23–20 Steelers grind lands right on the 3.

### Why the card leans this way
Experts split by number, not by team: Action Network and Brandon Anderson are on PIT at -3 or less, while Dave Tuley, Emerson Lotzia, Simon Hunter and Ross Tucker all need NO +3.5. Bets are even on the spread (55% bets / 56% money on NO), but the Saints moneyline draws 34% of the money from 20% of tickets, a sharp flag that matches the Friday drift from PIT -173 to -161. The synthesis passes the side at -3 and leans Under 44.5 (tier C, fair 43) on the wind. NO +3.5 is a SuperContest fill. The 10-team lottery ticket 740636102 carries Under 46.5 here.

### What breaks it
The wind forecast softens below about 30 km/h gusts by Sunday morning and the Under loses its reason. Or Elliott joins Echols on the sideline and Shough's passing game finds the gaps, which turns a Steelers field-goal win into a Saints cover.
