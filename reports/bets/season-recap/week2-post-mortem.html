Method and data notes

How this was graded

  • Leg results come from the ESPN box scores in data/fantasy/boxscores/ (16 finals). Scoring plays decide TD and first-TD legs. A player missing from the box score counts as a loss.
  • The AI record is docs/cards/2026-W02-sun-mon-card-draft.md (card v2) and docs/cards/2026-W02-props-card.md (BKR-priced props). There is no saved TNF card.
  • Round-robin returns are the Bookmaker settlements confirmed in the Week 2 analysis: $44.83 (dog-ML RR) and $16.39 (SC-fade RR).
  • The wagers file marks four MNF legs LOST that hit in the box score (Likely 4+ rec: 5; Newsome 3+ T+A: 5; Malachi Fields 27+ yds: 30 and 2+ rec: 2). None changes a ticket result.
