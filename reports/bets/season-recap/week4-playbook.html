How we bet matters more than what we bet

Week 4

The playbook

Lean in

Two-team round robins

3 of 4 cashed; $100 back on $88 (+14%). The only structure in profit.

Dog-ML RR every week; add the SuperContest 2-team RR you already planned.

Lean in

Underdogs on the moneyline

Dog MLs 8 of 21 (+19%) vs dog spreads 12 of 33 (-33%).

If you like a dog, take the ML (or the ML RR). No dog spreads in parlays.

Lean in

QB markets

Passing TDs 15 of 21 and QB INT thrown 8 of 12, both above break-even. 2+ TD on goal-line backs 4 of 5.

Anchor prop tickets on pass-TD overs and INT-yes for turnover-prone QBs.

Lean in

Props on the side you expect to win

Winning-team props 53 of 95 (+7%); losing-team props 42 of 91 (-9%).

Match every prop to your side read. Don't stack an offense you fade.

Lean in

Short tickets for the best reads

All six paying tickets were 1–2 legs or 2-team RRs.

Put the week's top read on a 1–2 leg ticket, not inside an 8-leg stack.

Lean in

AI prop screens

AI-backed props 31 of 54 (+19%); AI props you didn't place hit 35 of 72.

Keep using the AI prop screens, but take the legs straight or in short tickets.

Cut back

Long straight parlays

5+ legs: 58 tickets, $525, 0 cashed. The books' own prices said about 1.5 of them should have hit.

Cap 5+ leg tickets at $5 and 4 per week. Every extra leg costs about 9 cents per dollar at our leg edge.

Cut back

4-team master round robin

3 tickets, $315, $0 back (Week 3 has one $18 combo alive tonight).

Cut to $35 (70 × $0.50) or skip until the side picks improve.

Cut back

Receptions and receiving-yards legs

Receptions 20 of 46 (needed 59%); receiving yards 23 of 47. Receptions are one of two strong-evidence leaks.

No reception legs as filler. Only true target leaders on the side you expect to win.

Cut back

Full-game Unders in parlays

9 of 22 overall; Week 3 went 1 for 8. Totals have landed far from projections every week.

Unders only as a standalone or 2-leg ticket with a clear weather or QB reason.

Cut back

First TD, sacks, QB rushing

First TD 1 of 8, sacks 4 of 13, QB rushing yards 3 of 8.

Drop these from stacks. A first-TD lottery ticket at $5 max, if any.

Cut back

AI side picks without a second source

AI-backed sides and totals 33 of 83 (-26%); your own sides 24 of 43 (+9%).

An AI side only goes on a ticket if an independent source or your own read agrees.

Ticket rules

Leg price

−121 to −199 legs went 41 of 84 against ~59% needed. Plus-money +150 or longer is the only band in profit.

Avoid connector legs between −121 and −199. Prefer fewer, longer-priced legs.

Ticket rules

Leg count

At the season leg edge ($0.91 per $1 per leg), a 2-leg ticket returns about $0.83 and a 6-leg about $0.57.

Max 4 legs on anything over $5.

Ticket rules

Evidence bar

Only dog spreads and receptions clear a strong-evidence bar after 3 weeks. Everything else is thin or moderate.

Re-check these rules after Week 4 before sizing up any "lean in" item.

Method

How this was built

  • Sources: the Week 1–3 post-mortem datasets in reports/bets/week3-recap/ and reports/bets/season-recap/, graded from ESPN box scores. Week 3 excludes MNF (7 legs pending).
  • Category, price and origin cuts count each unique position once per week. Break-even and flat ROI use the leg price when the ticket recorded one (312 positions). Unpriced legs (mostly BEO multi-leg parlays and Bovada) count toward hit rates only.
  • Returns: Week 1 $80.56, Week 2 $131.24 (Bookmaker-confirmed), Week 3 $39.21 (dog-ML RR estimate from ticket prices). Promo-credit and free-bet tickets are excluded from stake.
  • "AI-backed" means the placed leg matched a saved AI recommendation. Week 1 and Week 2 AI records are partial, so "your own" sides include legs built with Claude in-session that were never saved.
  • Three weeks is a small sample. Treat every "lean in" item as provisional and re-grade after Week 4.
