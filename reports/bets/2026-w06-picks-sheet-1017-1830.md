**How to read "Confidence".** It is our estimated chance the lean cashes, pushes excluded. It comes from the Astra simulator where one was stated for the game, otherwise from the provisional quantitative model (60% market / 25% FTN / 15% SRS, not backtested), shaded where the Basis column says so. 50% is a coin flip; the break-even at -110 is 52.4%. Use it to rank picks against each other, not as a promise. Prop "Strength" (1-5) is a source-and-matchup rating, not a probability: no calibrated prop model exists.
