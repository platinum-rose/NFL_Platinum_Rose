## 📌 Executive Summary & Key Takeaways
In this episode of the Even Money podcast, Ross Tucker and Steve Fezzik delve into the intricacies of betting on the NFL preseason. They discuss the common misconceptions about preseason betting and emphasize the potential for profitability if approached with the right strategies. Steve outlines his "Preseason Betting Commandments," focusing on the minimal impact of home-field advantage, the importance of coaching tendencies, and the value of quarterback depth charts. They also highlight the significance of line movements and the potential for information-driven betting advantages. The episode underscores the importance of understanding team dynamics, such as quarterback competitions, and how these can influence betting outcomes. Additionally, they touch on the evolving nature of preseason games, with a trend towards higher scoring, and the implications for betting on totals.

## 📊 Exhaustive Ranking / Topic Breakdown

### Preseason Betting Commandments
- **Home Field Advantage**: Minimal impact in preseason, often overvalued in betting lines. Road teams catching 1 to 2.5 points are particularly valuable due to the potential for ties and the undervaluation of these spreads [^NOTE-1].
- **Coaching Tendencies**: Certain coaches, like Zac Taylor (Bengals) and Dan Quinn (Washington), have poor preseason records and should be avoided. Conversely, coaches with strong preseason records, like John Harbaugh, may offer betting opportunities, though recent trends show a decline in ATS success [^NOTE-2].
- **Quarterback Depth**: Teams with quarterback competitions or strong backup quarterbacks, such as the Eagles with Andy Dalton and Tanner McKee, are favorable bets. Mobile quarterbacks are particularly valuable in the second half of preseason games [^NOTE-3].

### Scoring Trends
- **Total Points**: Preseason games have been trending towards higher scoring, with the Hall of Fame game seeing five straight overs. Totals below 37 are generally favorable for betting the over due to the potential for unexpected scoring plays [^NOTE-4].

## 🎯 Expert Bets, Props & Market Positions

### Expert Wagers
- **Road Teams**: Favor road teams catching 1 to 2.5 points due to the minimal impact of home-field advantage and the potential for ties [^NOTE-5].
- **Overs on Low Totals**: Bet the over on games with totals below 37, as these games can easily exceed this number with a few unexpected plays [^NOTE-6].

### Pass
- **Avoid Betting on Certain Coaches**: Avoid betting on teams coached by Zac Taylor and Dan Quinn due to their poor preseason records [^NOTE-7].
