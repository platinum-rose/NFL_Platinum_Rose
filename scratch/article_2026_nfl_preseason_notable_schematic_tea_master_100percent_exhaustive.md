**Executive Summary**

The 2026 NFL preseason has revealed significant schematic shifts across several teams, with new coordinators implementing distinct strategies. Key changes include the Los Angeles Chargers' increased use of motion under Mike McDaniel, the Baltimore Ravens' heightened blitz rate with Jesse Minter, the Dallas Cowboys' diversified coverage schemes led by Christian Parker, the Pittsburgh Steelers' shift to 11 personnel under Mike McCarthy, and the Washington Commanders' increased under-center formations with David Blough. These adjustments are likely to influence regular-season performances and betting strategies.

**Team-by-Team & Player-by-Player Analytical Breakdown**

1. **Los Angeles Chargers**
   - **Scheme Change**: Increased use of motion.
   - **Motion Rate**: 88.9% of snaps in preseason, up from 64% last season (ranked 18th).
   - **Personnel**: Emphasis on plays with a receiver or tight end in motion.
   - **Coordinator**: Mike McDaniel, known for motion-heavy offense.

2. **Baltimore Ravens**
   - **Scheme Change**: Increased blitz rate.
   - **Blitz Rate**: 41.6% in preseason, up from 27.9% last season (ranked 15th).
   - **Late-Down Pressure**: 62.5% blitz rate on third or fourth downs.
   - **Key Players**: Kyle Hamilton, Roquan Smith.
   - **Coordinator**: Jesse Minter, previously tied for 12th in blitz rate with the Chargers.

3. **Dallas Cowboys**
   - **Scheme Change**: Diversified coverage schemes.
   - **Coverage Varieties**: Quarters, Cover 6, Cover 3, and Cover 3-Seam each used at least 17.4% of the time.
   - **Man Coverage Rate**: 11.6%, second-lowest in preseason.
   - **Coordinator**: Christian Parker, influenced by Vic Fangio's zone-heavy schemes.

4. **Pittsburgh Steelers**
   - **Scheme Change**: Shift to 11 personnel.
   - **Personnel Grouping Rates**:
     - 11 Personnel: 70.8% in preseason, up from 50.9%.
     - 12 Personnel: 14.6%, down from 29.9%.
     - 13 Personnel: 1.5%, down from 13.5%.
   - **Key Players**: D.K. Metcalf, Michael Pittman Jr., Germie Bernard.
   - **Coordinator**: Brian Angelichio, with Mike McCarthy's influence.

5. **Washington Commanders**
   - **Scheme Change**: Increased under-center formations.
   - **Under-Center Rate**: 47.9% in preseason, up from 10% over the last two years.
   - **Key Player**: Jayden Daniels, with limited previous experience under center.
   - **Coordinator**: David Blough, influenced by top offenses like the Rams, Bills, and Lions.

**Betting & Fantasy Rationale**

- **Chargers**: Expect a dynamic offense with high motion usage, potentially increasing scoring opportunities and fantasy value for Justin Herbert and receivers.
- **Ravens**: Increased blitzing may lead to more defensive turnovers and sacks, enhancing their fantasy defense value.

- **Steelers**: Emphasis on 11 personnel suggests increased passing volume, boosting the fantasy prospects of wide receivers.
