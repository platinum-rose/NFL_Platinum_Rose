## 2nd-half total as the hedge (Andy, 2026-11-15)
- Full-game Under L wins if 2H points <= L - 0.5 - (1H points). Books set the 2H total near half the pregame expectation (~22-24 here), largely regardless of 1H pace.
- Slow 1H (e.g. 13 at half): Under needs 2H <= 35; 2H Over ~23.5 creates a wide middle (24-35 wins both). Prefer the 2H Over.
- Normal 1H (~24): roughly a clean hedge.
- Fast 1H (e.g. 30): Under needs 2H <= 18; 2H Over 23.5 leaves a dead zone (19-23 loses both). Use the live full-game Over or an alt 2H line near (L - 1H points).
- Rule: compare the 2H line to (full-game line - 1H points). 2H line well below that = take the 2H Over (free middle); above it = stick with the live full-game Over.
