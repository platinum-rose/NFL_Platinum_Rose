5. Going forward: rest of the regular season

  1. Add a core split every week. Make one 3–4 leg split at roughly +300 to +600, built only from our strongest categories: QB INTs, game lines, rushing, and tackles where the model shows volume. Keep the 6–8 leg combos as small lottery tickets on the side.
  2. Cap exposure. No player-and-market on more than two tickets in a week, so one bad leg can't sink four tickets.
  3. Shift the mix. Fewer WR yardage overs and committee-back ATDs. More QB INTs, sides and totals, and lead-back rushing.
  4. Buy one notch lower on rushing and carries milestones when the price difference is small. This week that would have flipped several near misses.
  5. Watch blowout risk on tackles. Avoid tackle overs for defenders on big favorites (Lake on LAR −9.5): the other offense runs fewer plays.
  6. Track it weekly. We'll add an "Alejandro vs AI vs Andy" category table to each week's settlement, so these patterns get tested over more than one week.
