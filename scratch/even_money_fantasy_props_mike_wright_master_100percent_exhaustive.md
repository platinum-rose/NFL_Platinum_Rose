# 🏈 Even Money: Mike Wright: 2026 Fantasy Football Preview & Prop Bets

**Category:** Prio 3 - Best Bets & Props

## 📌 Executive Summary & Key Takeaways
In this episode of the Even Money podcast, Ross Tucker and Steve Fezik are joined by Mike Wright from the Fantasy Footballers to discuss the 2026 fantasy football landscape and prop bets. The discussion highlights the value of betting on player props, particularly unders, due to the inherent difficulty for sportsbooks to set accurate lines. Steve emphasizes the profitability of betting unders on season-long props, especially for skill positions, due to the high likelihood of injuries and missed games. Mike Wright shares insights on players he is bullish on, such as Tyler Shuck and Christian Watson, and those he is bearish on, like Quinshawn Judkins, due to various factors including team dynamics and coaching tendencies. The episode also covers preseason betting strategies, with Steve suggesting a contrarian approach by betting on teams that performed poorly in the previous week. The hosts also discuss the impact of rookie injuries on prop bets and the importance of not overreacting to preseason performances.

## 📊 Exhaustive Ranking / Topic Breakdown

### Player Analysis
- **Tyler Shuck (QB)**
  - High on Shuck due to his potential as a franchise quarterback and the presence of a true number one receiver in Chris Olave. Shuck's fast play style in Kellen Moore's offense is expected to boost his raw numbers [^NOTE-1].

- **Christian Watson (WR)**
  - Bullish on Watson as the de facto number one receiver due to the lack of depth in the receiving corps. Watson's performance in the second half of the previous season is promising [^NOTE-2].

- **Quinshawn Judkins (RB)**
  - Bearish on Judkins due to a potentially weaker defense, a poor offensive line, and uncertainty at the quarterback position. Todd Monken's tendency to split running back roles also factors into this analysis [^NOTE-3].

### Team & Scheme Analysis
- **Chargers**
  - Potential shift to more 12 personnel sets under McDaniel, impacting wide receiver usage [^NOTE-4].

- **Browns**
  - Concerns about the defense's ability to replicate last year's performance and the offensive line's struggles. The quarterback situation is also uncertain [^NOTE-5].

## 🎯 Expert Bets, Props & Market Positions

### EXPERT WAGER
- **Season-long Props (General)**
  - Bet unders on wide receivers, running backs, and quarterbacks due to the high likelihood of injuries and missed games [^NOTE-6].

### PROP BET
- **Tyler Shuck (QB)**
  - Consider betting on Shuck's passing yards over, given his potential in the Kellen Moore offense [^NOTE-7].

- **Christian Watson (WR)**

### PASS
