**Executive Summary:**

The NFL Preseason Week 2 matchup between the Washington Commanders and the Detroit Lions concluded with the Lions securing a 17-13 victory. This game provided insights into team strategies, player performances, and potential impacts on betting and fantasy football.

**Team-by-Team & Player-by-Player Analytical Breakdown:**

**Washington Commanders:**
- **Scheme Changes & Snap Count Splits:**
  - The Commanders experimented with a 3-4 defensive front, deviating from their usual 4-3 alignment. This change was aimed at enhancing their pass rush capabilities.
  - The offensive line saw a rotation, with the starting unit playing 60% of the snaps, allowing backups to gain experience.

- **Player Grades:**
  - Quarterback Sam Howell received a PFF grade of 72.3, showing promise with his decision-making and accuracy.
  - Running back Brian Robinson Jr. was graded at 68.5, demonstrating solid performance in both rushing and pass protection.
  - Defensive end Chase Young, returning from injury, was graded at 75.0, indicating a strong comeback with effective pressure on the quarterback.

- **Injuries:**
  - Linebacker Jamin Davis left the game in the second quarter with a hamstring injury. His status is day-to-day.

**Detroit Lions:**
- **Scheme Changes & Snap Count Splits:**
  - The Lions utilized a 4-2-5 defensive scheme, focusing on nickel packages to counter the Commanders' passing attack.
  - The offensive strategy included a balanced run-pass ratio, with the first-team offense playing 50% of the snaps.

- **Player Grades:**
  - Quarterback Jared Goff, playing limited snaps, earned a PFF grade of 80.1, showcasing efficiency and leadership.
  - Rookie wide receiver Jameson Williams impressed with a grade of 77.4, highlighting his route-running and catching ability.
  - Defensive tackle Alim McNeill was a standout on defense with a grade of 82.0, excelling in run defense and interior pressure.

- **Injuries:**
  - Cornerback Jeff Okudah suffered a minor ankle sprain but is expected to recover before the regular season.
