# 🏈 Sharp Football Analysis: NFL Preseason Week 1: Every Offense That Changed

**Category:** Prio 2 - Schemes & Tiers
**Source Episode ID:** 81b3f31e-c7b0-4821-a663-9cb6fb5944a7

## 📌 Executive Summary & Key Takeaways
In this episode of Sharp Football Analysis, Warren Sharp delves into the significant offensive changes observed during the first week of the NFL preseason. With 21 new offensive coordinators across the league, the focus is on how these changes manifest in early downs during the first half of games, providing insights into potential regular-season trends. The episode highlights seven teams with the most dramatic offensive shifts: Atlanta Falcons, Philadelphia Eagles, Miami Dolphins, Detroit Lions, Denver Broncos, LA Chargers, and Seattle Seahawks. Key trends include a league-wide decline in shotgun and motion rates, with several teams opting for more under-center plays to dictate defensive matchups. Notably, the Falcons and Eagles have drastically reduced their shotgun usage, while the Dolphins and Broncos have increased their air yards per attempt, indicating a more aggressive downfield approach. The episode also touches on personnel grouping changes, with teams like the Chargers and Raiders increasing their use of two-back and two-tight end sets, respectively. These insights provide a foundation for understanding how preseason strategies might translate into regular-season performance.

## 📊 Exhaustive Ranking / Topic Breakdown

### Atlanta Falcons
- Decreased shotgun rate from 78% to 46% on first half early downs, dropping from 3rd to 27th in the league [^NOTE-1].
- Increased 11 personnel usage from 36% to 92%, which would rank 1st in the NFL [^NOTE-2].
- Reduced pre-snap motion from 72% to 30%, the lowest in the NFL [^NOTE-3].
- Tua Tagovailoa's performance: 3 of 5 for 22 yards, 0.2 yards per attempt, 80% pressure rate [^NOTE-4].

### Philadelphia Eagles
- Shotgun rate decreased from 75% to 35%, which would rank 31st [^NOTE-5].
- Increased 12 personnel usage from 30% to 59% [^NOTE-6].
- Passes thrown at or behind the line of scrimmage increased from 22% to 50% [^NOTE-7].
- Andy Dalton: 0.7 air yards per attempt; Tanner McKee: 1.8 air yards per attempt [^NOTE-8].

### Miami Dolphins
- Air yards per attempt increased from 6.0 to 12.1, leading the NFL [^NOTE-9].
- Shotgun rate decreased from 68% to 28% [^NOTE-10].
- 12 personnel usage increased from 9.5% to 32% [^NOTE-11].

### Detroit Lions
- Increased three-plus wide receiver sets from 51% to 96% [^NOTE-12].
- Two tight end sets decreased to 3.8% [^NOTE-13].

### Denver Broncos
- Air yards per attempt increased from 7.1 to 13.8, a 94% increase [^NOTE-14].
- Passes thrown 10+ yards downfield increased from 27% to 55% [^NOTE-15].
- Three-plus wide receiver sets decreased from 65% to 44% [^NOTE-16].

### LA Chargers
- Two-plus running back sets increased from 35% to 77% [^NOTE-17].
- Air yards per attempt increased from 7.3 to 9.6 [^NOTE-18].
- Passes thrown 10+ yards downfield increased from 31% to 58% [^NOTE-19].

### Seattle Seahawks
- Air yards per attempt decreased from 8.1 to 2.9 [^NOTE-20].
- Passes thrown between the numbers increased from 48% to 83% [^NOTE-21].
