**Team-by-Team & Player-by-Player Analytical Breakdown:**

1. **Pittsburgh Steelers vs. Buffalo Bills (Thursday, Aug 27):**
   - **Pittsburgh Steelers:** Head coach Mike McCarthy is prioritizing extended looks for Will Howard and Drew Allar (combined 21-of-38 for 144 yards, 2 INT, 4 sacks in Week 2). Allar (2026 3rd-round pick) expected to lead the majority of drives; Mason Rudolph may see brief action; Aaron Rodgers will not dress.
   - **Buffalo Bills:** Joe Brady rested Josh Allen after intensive joint practices with Cleveland. Allen may see 1–2 series max, with Kyle Allen and Shane Buechele handling bulk work.

2. **New England Patriots vs. Cleveland Browns (Thursday, Aug 27):**
   - **Cleveland Browns:** Deshaun Watson officially named regular season starter; Todd Monken confirmed neither Watson nor Shedeur Sanders will play in the preseason finale. Dillon Gabriel and Taylen Green split the entire game in a direct battle for the emergency QB3 / practice squad role.
   - **New England Patriots:** Mike Vrabel highlighted roster depth concerns. Tommy DeVito needs extensive reps to secure the QB2 job behind Drake Maye, while Behren Morton also factors into late-game work.

3. **San Francisco 49ers vs. Las Vegas Raiders (Thursday, Aug 27):**
   - **Las Vegas Raiders:** Klint Kubiak has treated Kirk Cousins cautiously (one series all preseason). Cousins will sit or play one series; Fernando Mendoza (8-of-15, 86 yds, 1 INT) and Aidan O'Connell handle primary rotational volume, with Jacob Clark potentially seeing fourth-quarter work.
   - **San Francisco 49ers:** Brock Purdy played the opening drive last week (4-of-6); Kyle Shanahan is taking a cautious stance given injuries across the skill corps (George Kittle, Christian McCaffrey, Mike Evans, Christian Kirk).

4. **Los Angeles Rams vs. Los Angeles Chargers (Thursday, Aug 27):**
   - **Los Angeles Rams:** Sean McVay maintains his strict policy of resting all starters. Stetson Bennett IV (31 attempts in Wk 2) and Ty Simpson (12 attempts) will split halves in the competition for QB2 behind Matthew Stafford.
   - **Los Angeles Chargers:** Justin Herbert limited to one drive or held out entirely following offensive line injuries (including starting C Tyler Biadasz); Trey Lance and DJ Uiagalelei shoulder bulk volume.

5. **Washington Commanders vs. Baltimore Ravens (Friday, Aug 28):**
   - **Washington Commanders:** Jayden Daniels expected to sit after heavy reps in joint practices with Baltimore. Marcus Mariota remains sidelined (MCL sprain), leaving Athan Kaliakmanis and Sam Hartman to execute a conservative, vanilla gameplan.
   - **Baltimore Ravens:** Lamar Jackson (has not played preseason since 2021) sits. Tyler Huntley is entrenched at QB2, allowing Joe Fagnano (48 passes through 2 weeks) to command extended playing time.

6. **Atlanta Falcons vs. Miami Dolphins (Friday, Aug 28):**
   - **Atlanta Falcons:** Kevin Stefanski designated Week 3 as a targeted dress rehearsal for starting skill units, though Michael Penix Jr. is unlikely to suit up due to a lingering camp injury. Jack Strand and Cooper Rush (combined 22-of-28, 297 yds, 1 TD in Wk 2) will take significant snaps.
   - **Miami Dolphins:** Offense has generated just 10 points and 102 total yards through two exhibition games under Jeff Hafley. Malik Willis starts for 1–2 series before giving way to Cam Miller and Mark Gronowski; Quinn Ewers is limited by a groin injury.

7. **Houston Texans vs. Carolina Panthers (Friday, Aug 28):**
   - **Houston Texans:** CJ Stroud expected to direct an opening scoring drive before resting; Davis Mills and Brett Rypien take over with Graham Mertz injured.
   - **Carolina Panthers:** Haynes King progressing toward Week 1; Bryce Young sees 1–2 series; Kyle Trask and Kenny Pickett manage the remainder.

8. **New York Giants vs. New York Jets (Friday, Aug 28):**
   - **New York Giants:** Jaxson Dart held out; veteran Jameis Winston and Brandon Allen share the workload alongside Jake Haener.
   - **New York Jets:** Geno Smith played one drive in Week 2; Cade Klubnik, Bailey Zappe, and Brady Cook compete for the backup hierarchy.

9. **Tampa Bay Buccaneers vs. Jacksonville Jaguars (Friday, Aug 28):**
   - **Tampa Bay Buccaneers:** Baker Mayfield limited/rested; Jake Browning and Jalon Daniels drive the offensive rotation.
