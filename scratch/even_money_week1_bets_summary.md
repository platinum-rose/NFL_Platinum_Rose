### 📐 Master Betting Principles Emphasized
1. **The Mathematical Chasm Between +3 and +3.5:**  
   Fezzik broke down why pro handicappers obsess over the half-point hook: assuming a true 3-point game, favorites cover 45% of the time and dogs cover/win 45% of the time, with exactly 3 landing 10% of the time. Betting +3 results in a 45–45 record with 10 pushes (bleeding juice). Betting +3.5 converts all 10 pushes into wins, turning the record into **55–45 (55% win rate)**—the benchmark of elite professional sports betting.
2. **September Totals & Offensive Lag:**  
   Because modern collective bargaining agreements limit live contact in training camps, NFL defenses regularly start the season ahead of offensive timing and chemistry. Totals above 50 in Week 1 strongly trend toward the Under.
3. **Teaser Market Pricing Standards:**  
   Tucker and Fezzik established **-125** as their benchmark fair price for 6-point, two-team NFL teasers, warning recreational bettors against accepting predatory -135 pricing while advising listeners to hunt for -120 or -110 where available.
