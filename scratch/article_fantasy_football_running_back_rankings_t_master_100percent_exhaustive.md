However, the Lions' offensive line has dropped from second to 13th in PFF run-blocking grade, with two starters replaced this offseason. - **Atlanta Falcons:**
  - **Bijan Robinson:** Ranked second, Robinson has consistently been a top-three fantasy running back, with over 1,400 rushing yards and at least 60 receptions each year. The replacement of Tyler Allgeier with Brian Robinson Jr. is seen as a lateral move for his goal-line prospects. Kevin Stefanski's history of utilizing a lead back is favorable for Robinson. Concerns exist regarding the offensive line, with Jake Matthews' career-low PFF run-blocking grade and Kaleb McGary's retirement leading to the signing of Jawaan Taylor, who is more of a pass protector.
